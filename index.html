Q&A

We help homeowners with a wide range of practical repair, installation, and improvement projects. That includes TV mounting, furniture assembly, shelving and closet systems, fence repair, interior and exterior painting, trim work, drywall repair, window shade installation, and simple household needs such as changing light bulbs or air filters. Whether it is a single repair or a list of projects that need attention, we work to help make the home more functional, finished, and well cared for.
We keep all work within the scope of the licensing requirements that apply to our services. Accordingly, we do not take on larger projects that fall outside that scope, and we do not perform structural work, work inside walls, electrical work, or major plumbing. We are committed to clear communication, professional service, and operating within the proper legal standards on every job.
